1965 · Berkshire Hathaway
Took control of a failing textile mill
Transformed into a giant
→ Pivoted from textiles to insurance & investments
→ Subsidiaries: GEICO, BNSF Railway, Dairy Queen
→ BRK.A share price: $8 (1965) → $600,000+ (2024)
→ Earned nickname: "The Oracle of Omaha"
Investment philosophy
Value investing
Buy great companies at fair prices — not cheap companies at any price
Economic moat
Seek durable competitive advantages: brand, network, low cost
Long-term holding
"Favorite holding period is forever" — patience over trading
Circle of competence
Only invest in businesses you truly understand
Margin of safety
Always buy below intrinsic value — protect the downside first
Power of compounding
Reinvest returns early and consistently — time is your greatest asset
